Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026 · iPhone app: com.parallelcalculator.app · Android app: com.calcboard.app

Parallel Calculator does not collect, store or share any of your data. It works fully offline, needs no account, and your calculations never leave your device unless you send them somewhere yourself.

Who provides Parallel Calculator

Parallel Calculator (called CalcBoard on Android) is made by ENTR0ZY. For privacy questions or support, email entrozy@protonmail.com.

Information used on your device

The app uses the numbers, expressions, row names, units and board names you enter to work out your calculations. It saves your boards, calculation history, undo and redo steps, and settings in the app's own private storage on your device, so your work is still there after you close the app. We can't see any of it.

No collection, sharing or tracking

  • The app makes no network requests and sends nothing to ENTR0ZY or to any server.
  • No ads, no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising identifier and no tracking of any kind.
  • No account or sign-in.

Copy and Share

When you choose Copy, the app puts the chosen result on your device's clipboard. When you choose Share, it turns your board into text for your device's share sheet. Anything you send to another app or person is handled under that destination's own privacy practices.

Permissions

The app uses vibration for touch feedback, which you can turn off in the app. It does not ask for camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos or notification access.

Keeping and deleting your data

Saved data stays on your device until you delete it or remove the app. Deleting the app removes everything it stored. There is no cloud sync or backup by the app, so we can't recover deleted boards. Text you copied or shared has to be removed from the clipboard or from where you sent it.

Children

Parallel Calculator does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Emailing support

If you email us, we receive your email address, your message and any attachments, and use them only to reply. Please don't send private calculations unless they are needed to explain a problem.

Purchases

Purchases and refunds are handled by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play) under their own terms.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
