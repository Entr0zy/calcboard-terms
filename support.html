Support

Something not working, or a question? Email entrozy@protonmail.com and you'll get a reply, usually within two days. It helps to say which device you're on.

Common questions

How do I combine results? Type a calculation in any row and press =. Tap a row's result (or drag it up) to add it to the Master calculation at the top, then use the operator buttons to join results.

Can one row use another row's result? Yes. While typing in a row, tap another row's name (A, B, C…) to insert its result. It updates when that row changes.

How do I read a number in words? Press and hold any result.

How do I add a unit like £ or kg? Open the row's ⋯ menu and choose Unit.

Can I keep separate sets of calculations? Yes. Tap the board name at the top of the Master calculation to create, rename, switch or delete boards.

Is my work backed up? No. Everything stays on your device only. Use Share to send a copy of a board somewhere safe.

Does it need the internet? No. Parallel Calculator works fully offline.
